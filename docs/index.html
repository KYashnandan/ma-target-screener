What it does

How it works

  1. Take 20 companies acquired in 2021-23 and 20 comparable ones that stayed independent.
  2. Pull each company's financials from SEC EDGAR for the year before the deal.
  3. Collect that year's Google News headlines and score each with FinBERT.
  4. Combine 7 financial and 4 sentiment features and train XGBoost.
  5. Score every company out-of-fold and rank them.
